## Agent Instructions

- Configure Neovim with privacy as a hard requirement: no telemetry, analytics, remote logging, cloud AI/completion, or uploading of file names, paths, buffer contents, diagnostics, snippets, or project metadata. Prefer local-only LSPs, formatters, linters, search, and explicit/manual installs; exclude anything that may contact third-party services unless clearly disabled and documented.
- Do not commit unless explicitly asked.
- Use `rg` for searching.
